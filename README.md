✨ ABOUT PRIYO CHAT

Priyo Chat is an Android communication project with a simple vision:

To make connecting with people easier, more convenient, and more enjoyable.

We believe communication should bring people closer, no matter where they are.

Our mission is to build a modern messaging experience with simplicity, accessibility, and reliability at its heart.
